# Surface brief — src/pages/index.astro

Visitor mode: Persuade. One primary action, everywhere: JOIN THE WAITLIST (scrolls to the waitlist panel). Secondary actions: EXPLORE AKIPOLY (scrolls to the board showcase). Web3 kept as background detail, never the hook, per the confirmed audience.

Audience: two sides of the GameFi fence — property-trading board-game lovers and GameFi players, converting on the same promise of nostalgia + strategy + arcade fun.

Constraints: no backend (front-end waitlist with an integration seam), no invented traction/handles/dates, design.md binding, copy human and trademark-free, reduced-motion respected.

Mode-specific strategy: visitor decides in seconds whether this is fun or forgettable. The first viewport must sell the feeling of the roll — nostalgia for the tabletop, arcade energy, zero corporate gloss — and route every path to the one CTA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Akipoly is the property-trading tabletop reborn as an arcade GameFi — the page sells the feeling of the roll, not a feature list. It refuses the category default (gradient-blob crypto hero, orb, LED-grid "decentralized" gloss) and the soft pastel gamification app page.

OWN-WORLD: Warm Ivory (#FFFDF2) ground with a faint ink dot grid; Electric Yellow (#FFF500) reserved for the primary CTA and the hero headline plate; flat white cards and panels with 3px ink borders and 5px hard offset shadows; Periwinkle (#9DB7FB) for the secondary CTA and backdrop shapes; Tangerine, Lime Mint, Bubblegum Pink and Electric Violet used sparingly — never all four in one component; Archivo Black display type with tight leading and one rotated yellow highlight plate; Space Grotesk for everything else; a hand-authored SVG world (tile-ring board, a leaning die showing five, a rotated "WAITLIST OPEN" sticker, sparkles, star polygons) recurring in hero and showcase; sticker/squiggle/dotted details; yellow ticket-tape marquee spelling BUY BUILD COMPETE TRADE ROLL OWN.

STORY: Visitor lands → reads two stacked Archivo Black statements (ROLL THE DICE. / OWN THE BOARD.) and a nine-word promise → registers the die leaning on the board as "this is the property-trading game, rebuilt loud" → scrolls through three verbs (BUY / BUILD / COMPETE) that name the loop without rules → sees the concept board ("mockups, not screenshots") → hits GET FIRST DIBS → joins with one email field → gets a real success state with a share button.
